❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our artificial vertical gardens

What are the benefits of artificial vertical gardens? ▼

Artificial vertical gardens offer numerous benefits including:

  • No maintenance required - no watering or pruning needed
  • Lasts for 5+ years with proper care
  • UV-protected designs for outdoor use
  • Cost-effective compared to living plants
  • Perfect for spaces with low natural light
  • Professional appearance for offices and homes
Are these panels suitable for outdoor use? ▼

Yes! Our artificial vertical garden panels are designed with UV protection to withstand outdoor conditions. The UV-protective coating ensures they resist fading and degradation from sun exposure. However, we recommend placing them in areas with partial shade for optimal longevity.

How long does installation take? ▼

Installation time varies based on the size and complexity of your project:

  • Small panels (single unit): 1-2 hours
  • Medium walls (4-6 panels): 3-4 hours
  • Large installations: Full day or more

Our professional team handles all installation for you if you opt for our installation service.

What's the warranty on these products? ▼

All our artificial vertical garden panels come with a 2-year manufacturer's warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Extended warranty options are available for purchase. Regular maintenance and proper care ensure your panels last even longer.

Can I customize the panels to match my décor? ▼

Absolutely! We offer customization options including:

  • Different plant species combinations
  • Color variations and themes
  • Custom sizing for special spaces
  • Arrangement based on your preferences

Contact our team to discuss your specific customization needs.

What's the minimum order quantity? ▼

We offer flexibility with minimum orders:

  • Individual panels available (1 piece minimum)
  • Bulk discounts for 5+ panels
  • Special rates for B2B and commercial projects

Contact us for wholesale pricing and corporate orders.
